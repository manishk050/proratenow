Privacy

ProCalc performs its calculator logic in your browser. Calculator inputs and your theme preference are stored in your browser's localStorage so they can be restored after a refresh.

The calculator code does not require an account or backend database and does not intentionally transmit the amounts or dates you enter to a ProCalc application server.

Hosting logs

Your hosting provider may process standard technical request information such as IP address, browser type, requested URL, and timestamps for security and service operation. Check your hosting provider's policy for details.

Future analytics or advertising

This version does not include advertising or analytics scripts. If analytics, advertising, or affiliate technologies are added later, update this page and any consent controls before deployment.
