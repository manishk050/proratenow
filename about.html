About ProCalc

ProCalc is a free browser-based tool for calculating partial-month rent and salary with an actual calendar-day method.

Methodology

For the selected month, ProCalc determines the exact number of calendar days, including leap-year February. It divides the monthly amount by that day count to get a daily rate, intersects the selected date range with the target month, counts both the first and last active day, and multiplies the daily rate by the active day count.

Dates outside the selected month are not counted. A date range with no overlap returns zero. A range covering the full month returns the full monthly amount.

Important limitation

Some leases and employers use a 30-day divisor, working days, hourly rates, payroll calendars, or jurisdiction-specific rules. ProCalc is a mathematical estimate, not legal, tax, or payroll advice.
